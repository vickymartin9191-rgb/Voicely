The problem

Your front desk is your biggest bottleneck.

Most dental clinics lose patients before they ever walk through the door. Here's exactly where it happens.

Missed calls = missed patients

When your front desk is with a patient, the phone rings out. That caller doesn't leave a voicemail — they call the next clinic on the list. You never know what you lost.

Manual scheduling drains your team

Every call your front desk handles is 4–8 minutes off patient care. Multiply that by 30 calls a day and you're losing hours of productive capacity every single week.

Generic websites don't rank or convert

Template sites built for any business rank poorly in local search and give patients no reason to choose you. The clinic down the street with a better site wins by default.
